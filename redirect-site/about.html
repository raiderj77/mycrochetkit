ABOUT

One place for the project. One place for the math.

MyCrochetKit is a small, private workspace for keeping the details of an individual crochet project together. It stores planner information in your browser and does not require an account.

FiberTools is the companion calculator and reference site. That separation is deliberate: MyCrochetKit organizes your work, while FiberTools handles yarn estimates, gauge adjustments, size calculations, conversions, and reference material.

Why it works this way

A planner does not need your identity or a cloud account to be useful. Keeping the first version browser-local makes it simpler, faster, and more private. Exported backups let you move or protect the information yourself.

What it does not do

MyCrochetKit does not host copied patterns, publish projects automatically, upload your notes, or promise that browser storage is a permanent backup. If a project matters, export a backup.
