PRIVACY

Your project information stays in your browser.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Planner data

The planner saves the information you enter using local browser storage on your device. MyCrochetKit does not transmit that planner data to a MyCrochetKit server, cloud database, analytics service, or advertising platform.

Accounts and tracking

The current planner does not offer accounts, use cookies, load advertising, or include analytics and behavioral tracking scripts.

Exports and deletion

You can export a JSON backup from the planner. That file is created by your browser and downloaded to your device. You can remove an individual project in the planner. Clearing site data for mycrochetkit.com in your browser will remove all locally saved projects from that browser.

Links to FiberTools

MyCrochetKit links to FiberTools calculators and references. After following a link, FiberTools’ own privacy terms apply. A standard web referrer may identify mycrochetkit.com as the site that sent the visit, but it does not contain the project information stored in the planner.

Important limitation

Browser storage is not guaranteed permanent. It can be removed when you clear browser data, use private browsing, change devices, or reset a browser profile. Export backups for projects you want to keep.
